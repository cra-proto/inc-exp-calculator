Monthly net income and expense worksheet

You can use this worksheet to determine how much you are able to pay toward your CRA balance or other government debts collected by the CRA, based on your current monthly income and expenses.

You can complete the worksheet online for individuals or businesses. Use your results when setting up a payment arrangement online or when speaking with a collections officer.

If you don’t need to calculate the amount and just want to make a payment or set up an arrangement to make smaller payments over time, refer to: Payments to the CRA.

Choose your calculator

Disclaimer: Accuracy depends on your input

This calculator is available for your convenience. The reliability of the results depends on the accuracy of the information you enter.

Privacy statement: We do not record any of the data you enter in the calculator

Remember to clear your Internet browser history and close the browser after you are finished to protect your information, particularly if you are using a public computer. Although we do not keep any of the data you enter, the data does stay on your computer. Information stored on your computer could be accessed by others.

Choose the option that applies to you to help determine your ability to pay. The calculator will total your income and expenses and show your monthly net amount below.

Once you complete the worksheet, you can use the results to schedule a series of payments in My Account or My Business Account, or when you speak with your assigned collections officer.

You can also print your results for your records.

Schedule a series of payments

The payment arrangement calculator is available on the My Account and My Business Account secure portals. To see if you can use this option to pay your debt, go to Pay by scheduled pre-authorized debit (PAD) through CRA online services.

To see different payment schedules to pay your debt over time:

  1. Sign in to your CRA account
  2. Select your Individual account or your Business account
  3. Select "Proceed to pay"
  4. Select "Schedule a series of payments"
  5. Complete the fields on the screen and select "Calculate"

Any amount you owe is payable immediately upon (re)assessment. If you cannot pay in full immediately, you can use the results of this worksheet to set up a pre-authorized debit (PAD) agreement to address your obligations. Be realistic when using these tools and think about all factors that may affect your ability to continue payments for the entire period. PAD agreements may be subject to periodic reviews and you may be contacted by the CRA. You must adhere to your PAD agreement, continue to file all returns on time, and stay up to date with your obligations.

If you are not able to schedule a series of payments online, you should contact us to set up a payment arrangement.
